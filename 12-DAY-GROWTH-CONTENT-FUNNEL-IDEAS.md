# ProofTTL — 12-Day Business / Content / Funnel / Growth / Ideas Execution Plan

## Mission

In 12 days, ProofTTL should stop feeling like a landing page for a verification service and start demonstrating a distinct verification product.

The product moment to optimize for:

> Paste an AI answer, article, report, or set of claims. ProofTTL finds the factual claims that matter, stress-tests them against evidence, deliberately tries to disprove them, and produces an inspectable verification record.

Primary verdicts remain `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, and `UNKNOWN`.

The category is **independent claim verification for consequential decisions**. “AI fact checker” is an acquisition term, not the product boundary.

---

## 1. BUSINESS

### Commercial wedge

Do not initially sell truth checking to everyone. Sell protection from expensive factual mistakes.

Initial buyer wedges:

1. AI-generated reports before clients or executives see them.
2. Marketing and website claims before publication.
3. Vendor/product claims before purchase decisions.
4. Pitch-deck statistics before investor distribution.
5. Citation/research verification before publication.

### Commercial promise

A buyer should leave knowing:

- the exact proposition tested;
- the strongest evidence supporting it;
- the strongest evidence challenging it;
- why evidence was weighted the way it was;
- what remains unknown;
- when the conclusion may become stale;
- what ProofTTL tried to find to defeat the verdict;
- how another person can inspect the work.

### Product ladder

**Free / immediate:** Claim Stress Test — demonstrate risk and identify claims worth deeper investigation.

**Paid:** Independent verification of consequential claims with deeper adversarial research and a durable verification artifact.

**Existing commercial anchor:** 3–5 critical claims / Claim Stress Test service at $129. Preserve current truthful promises while the automated experience is built.

## 2. CONTENT

Every content surface should feed the same user behavior:

> I have information I may rely on → I am not fully confident in it → ProofTTL can stress-test the claims.

### Core messages

- Don’t trust the answer. Stress-test the claims.
- Check the sentence, not the confidence.
- Evidence for. Evidence against. No manufactured certainty.
- AI-generated does not mean AI-verified.
- UNKNOWN is a valid result when evidence does not justify certainty.
- Every verification has a freshness boundary.
- Verification should be inspectable, challengeable, and reproducible.
- ProofTTL is for claims that are expensive to get wrong.

## 3. FUNNEL

### Discovery

Google / AI discovery / GitHub / external mention / shared verification record.

### Landing

Above the fold should communicate the action before terminology:

> Don’t trust the answer. Stress-test the claims.
>
> Paste AI output, research, or a report. ProofTTL identifies the factual claims worth checking and tests them against evidence.

Primary action: paste text / URL / document and start the Stress Test.

Avoid forcing account creation or a pricing decision before demonstrating value where technically possible.

### Activation moment

The experience should rapidly transform a large input into a claim-level risk summary, for example:

- 23 factual claims detected
- 7 worth verifying
- 2 potentially high-impact
- 4 supported
- 1 contradicted
- 2 unknown

The most interesting failure or uncertainty should be surfaced immediately.

### Conversion

Free result demonstrates the problem. Paid independent verification deepens the work.

Potential paid value, only as implemented:

- deeper adversarial research;
- source-quality analysis;
- evidence for and against;
- signed Fact Lease;
- durable/shareable verification record;
- monitoring/recheck behavior.

Conversion framing:

> These claims are going into something important? Independently verify the consequential ones before you publish, present, buy, recommend, or rely on them.

### Funnel metric

The key activation metric is not raw traffic. It is:

> How quickly can ProofTTL make a new visitor discover a factual claim they are no longer comfortable relying on without verification?

Track later:

- visitor → input submitted;
- input → claims extracted;
- extracted → consequential claim opened;
- claim opened → evidence inspected;
- evidence inspected → share or paid intent;
- paid intent → purchase.

## 4. GROWTH

### Product-led distribution

Every public-safe verification should eventually be capable of becoming a durable verification page with:

- exact claim;
- verdict;
- evidence mapping;
- observation time;
- TTL/recheck state;
- methodology;
- version/correction history;
- verification identifier.

The loop:

verification → artifact → sharing/indexing → discovery → new Stress Test → paid verification → more artifacts.

### Shareable verification receipt

Create a compact portable representation of a verification containing:

- claim or short claim identifier;
- verdict;
- checked date;
- evidence count where meaningful;
- Fact Lease / verification ID;
- canonical verification location.

Do not reduce complex verification to a misleading badge of permanent truth.

### Strategic open-source / research opportunity

Consider a small public benchmark rather than open-sourcing the moat.

Working concept: **ProofTTL ClaimBench**.

Test whether verification systems catch:

- stale evidence;
- scope mismatches;
- technically true but misleading claims;
- real citations that do not entail the attached sentence;
- circular sourcing;
- denominator/population errors;
- changed product/pricing/certification facts;
- contradictory primary sources;
- unsupported certainty.

This can provide GitHub/research visibility while establishing ProofTTL’s verification philosophy.

### Authority content

Prefer experiments over self-congratulatory competitor pages.

Examples:

- Give multiple AI fact checkers a claim supported only by stale evidence and measure what happens.
- Test whether systems distinguish a real citation from a citation that actually supports the sentence.
- Mutate one material word in a set of claims and measure whether verification catches the changed scope.

Any published benchmark must use reproducible methodology and fair descriptions of competitors.

### Machine discovery

Continue strengthening canonical definitions and machine-readable surfaces around distinct ProofTTL concepts such as:

- Claim Contract
- Fact Lease
- Evidence TTL
- Claim Stress Test
- Contradiction Pass
- Citation Entailment

Definitions must describe implemented concepts rather than invented capabilities.

## 5. PRODUCT IDEAS

### Red Team My Answer

Signature interaction: after a provisional verdict, ProofTTL deliberately tries to defeat it.

Possible visible states:

- checking newer evidence;
- searching for exceptions;
- checking original source;
- testing date/scope boundary;
- looking for contradictory primary evidence.

The red-team pass must be a real separate research operation, not an animation over the same answer.

### Claim Blast Radius

Rank claims by consequence and dependency rather than treating every factual sentence equally. Identify which claims materially support the document’s conclusion or decision.

### Citation Autopsy

For cited AI output, separate:

1. Does the citation exist?
2. Is it the claimed source?
3. Is it current enough?
4. Does it actually support the attached proposition?
5. Is the support full, partial, contextual-only, contradictory, or irrelevant?
6. Is there a stronger primary source?

### Truth Decay

Operationalize TTL. Display source observation time, volatility, recommended recheck date, and invalidation conditions. Avoid pretending all claims decay at the same rate.

### Evidence Battle

Present strongest evidence FOR and strongest evidence AGAINST side by side, followed by the reason the final verdict weighs one side more heavily or remains UNKNOWN.

### Source Genealogy

Detect when many citations derive from the same underlying source. Distinguish citation count from independent evidence origins.

Example concept: “7 citations, 2 independent evidence origins.”

### Scope Trap Detector

Look for support that fails because of date, geography, product version, plan tier, population, denominator, definition, jurisdiction, or comparison class.

### Evidence Ceiling

Stop searching when public evidence cannot establish the proposition. State what missing evidence would be required to resolve it.

### Claim Mutation Test

Probe the boundary of a claim by varying material qualifiers such as date, quantity, scope, entity, and geography. This is a later feature unless it directly strengthens the core 12-day demo.

### ProofTTL Challenge

Allow a verification recipient to submit contradictory evidence. Valid challenges can reopen a verification and become part of its version history. Later-phase feature.

### Verification Diff

When monitored evidence changes, show what changed between versions rather than merely saying that something changed.

### What Would Change My Verdict?

Every result should eventually state what evidence could flip or resolve the verdict. Especially important for UNKNOWN.

## 6. SIGNATURE UI: THE PROOF GRAPH

Long-term interface direction: verification should be understandable as a graph rather than only a prose report.

Center node: exact claim.

Possible edges/nodes:

- SUPPORTS
- PARTIALLY SUPPORTS
- CONTRADICTS
- DERIVED FROM
- COPIED FROM
- STALE
- SCOPE MISMATCH
- SOURCE CONFLICT
- CHALLENGE

The graph should make the adversarial process visible. A provisional verdict can be attacked by explicit challenge branches before the final verdict is issued.

This gives Claim Contracts, source genealogy, evidence hierarchy, contradiction passes, TTL, and Fact Leases a shared visual model.

Do not spend the 12-day sprint building an elaborate graph renderer before the underlying verification data is trustworthy. A simple inspectable representation is enough initially.

## 7. 12-DAY EXECUTION

### Days 1–3 — Core verification mechanics

- Define the Claim Contract schema.
- Implement/strengthen long-text claim extraction.
- Add claim-worthiness / materiality ranking.
- Define evidence object and source-quality fields.
- Separate citation existence from claim entailment.
- Preserve `SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / UNKNOWN` as top-level verdicts.

### Days 4–6 — Adversarial engine

- Implement explicit evidence-for and evidence-against retrieval/mapping.
- Implement a separate contradiction pass.
- Add scope/date/exception checks.
- Add source independence/genealogy signals where feasible.
- Add TTL/recheck fields.
- Ensure UNKNOWN explains what is missing and what could resolve it.

### Days 7–8 — Killer experience

- Build the Claim Stress Test input → extraction → ranked claims → verification flow.
- Surface the most consequential contradiction/uncertainty quickly.
- Build a simple Proof Graph / evidence battle view from real verification data.
- Generate a durable verification/Fact Lease artifact from completed work.

### Days 9–10 — Proof and trust

- Upgrade the public sample audit to demonstrate the real mechanics.
- Publish/upgrade methodology.
- Publish source hierarchy and correction/version policy.
- Update `/ai-fact-checker/` around the implemented product distinction.
- Add one strong citation-verification use case if the underlying mechanism works.

### Days 11–12 — Distribution

- Build/share one reproducible demonstration that exposes a subtle verification failure.
- Strengthen high-intent search surfaces around actual implemented capabilities.
- Make verification artifacts shareable/indexable where safe.
- Strengthen GitHub documentation and machine-readable discovery surfaces.
- Submit/update discovery surfaces after production verification.
- Focus outreach/content on the demo rather than generic product announcements.

## 8. SCOPE DISCIPLINE

Do not let these consume the sprint unless the core experience is already strong:

- live-stream verification;
- elaborate multimodal pipelines;
- browser extensions;
- giant integrations;
- automatic rewriting;
- complex dashboards;
- full public challenge network;
- polished graph visualization before graph data exists;
- vanity SEO page volume.

The 12-day build is successful if a stranger can paste a substantial AI answer and quickly understand:

1. what factual claims it contains;
2. which claims matter most;
3. what evidence supports them;
4. what evidence challenges them;
5. whether the citations actually entail the claims;
6. what ProofTTL did to try to disprove the preliminary result;
7. what remains unknown;
8. when the evidence may become stale;
9. how to inspect the resulting verification record.

The memorable behavior should be:

> Before you rely on that, ProofTTL it.
