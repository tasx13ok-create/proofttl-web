# ProofTTL — Regulatory Timing Finding

## Complyze: EU AI Act high-risk deadline is shown as already live

**Observed on:** Complyze public Live Regulatory Feed
**Risk:** High
**Verdict:** Contradicted / stale

### What the product says

Complyze's August 2, 2026 EU AI Act feed item states that **high-risk AI systems must now comply with conformity-assessment requirements**.

### What the current law says

The consolidated EU AI Act, Article 113, after the July 27, 2026 amendment, applies the relevant high-risk framework later:

- **December 2, 2027** — Article 6(2) / Annex III high-risk systems
- **August 2, 2028** — Article 6(1) / Annex I product high-risk systems

Primary authority: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, consolidated 2026-07-27
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng

European Commission implementation guidance confirms the same phased dates:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/navigating-ai-act

### Why this matters

This is not a cosmetic date mismatch. A compliance user could reasonably read the feed as saying conformity-assessment obligations are already operative today, when the current law gives the main high-risk categories later application dates.

### Recommended correction

Describe August 2, 2026 as a major AI Act application/enforcement milestone, then show the delayed high-risk dates separately instead of saying high-risk systems must comply now.
