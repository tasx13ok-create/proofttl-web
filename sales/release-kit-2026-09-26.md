## Post 2 — Evidence hygiene (20–25 second Reel)
Voiceover:
“A link is not the end of a fact-check. Open it. Find the sentence that supports the claim. Check the date. Check who it applies to. Then look for evidence against it. If those steps do not support the wording, narrow the wording. A real citation can still support the wrong conclusion.”

Visual sequence:
0–4s: One sentence beside a citation; title: “The link exists.”
4–9s: Highlight the exact supporting passage; title: “Does the evidence match?”
9–15s: Date, population, and conditions appear one at a time.
15–20s: A second source challenges one clause; keep the conflict visible.
20–25s: “Evidence > confidence.” Then ProofTTL symbol.

Use clearly labeled illustrative text. Do not invent paper titles, statistics, screenshots, or source quotations.

Caption:
Before you reuse an AI answer, check what the cited source actually establishes.

Exact claim. Exact evidence. Visible limits.

4. “This result applies to everyone.” The source may describe a narrow group.
5. Start with the claim someone will act on. Then inspect the evidence.
